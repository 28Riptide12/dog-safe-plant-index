## Quick Start

1. **Browse Plants**: Visit the Plant Catalogue to see 609 dog-safe plants
2. **Search**: Use the search bar to find plants by name or scientific name
3. **Filter**: Apply filters for safety status, season, or climate zone
4. **Save Favorites**: Click the ⭐ star icon to save plants you like
5. **Track Your Garden**: Add plants to "My Plant Library" to monitor care needs

## Plant Catalogue

### Layout
The plant catalogue displays plants in a grid with:
- **Plant Image**: High-quality photo of the plant
- **Plant Name**: Common name (e.g., "Rose")
- **Scientific Name**: Latin name (e.g., "Rosa spp.")
- **Description**: Brief overview of the plant
- **Safety Status**: Badge showing toxicity level
- **Find Correct Photo**: Button to update plant image if needed

### Pagination
- **12 plants per page** by default
- Navigate using Previous/Next buttons
- Jump to specific page using page number buttons
- Smart page navigation (shows only nearby pages on mobile)

### Plant Safety Levels

| Badge | Meaning | Safety |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Non-toxic to dogs | ✅ SAFE | No worries - fully safe for dogs |
| May be Toxic | ⚠️ CAUTION | Safe in moderation - avoid large quantities |
| Toxic | ❌ DANGER | Avoid completely - can harm dogs |

## My Plant Library

### Purpose
Track plants you're currently growing at home with:
- Plant location in your garden (e.g., "Front border", "Patio pot")
- Health status (healthy, needs attention, recovering)
- Personal care notes and observations
- Automatic care reminders

### Adding a Plant

1. Click **"+ Add Plant"** button
2. Search for the plant by name
3. Select the plant from results
4. Fill in details:
   - **Location**: Where is it planted? (e.g., "South-facing bed")
   - **Health Status**: Current condition
   - **Notes**: Your observations
5. Click **"Save Plant"**
6. System automatically creates a watering reminder

### Care Schedule

The system tracks:
- **Watering**: Default 7-day intervals (adjustable)
- **Pruning**: Based on plant type
- **Fertilizing**: Seasonal recommendations
- **Health checks**: Monthly inspections

### Viewing Care Tasks

Tasks appear in the **Care Schedule** section sorted by:
- Urgent (overdue tasks at top)
- Upcoming (within 7 days)
- By plant name

### Marking Tasks Complete

- Click a task to mark it done
- System resets the timer for next occurrence
- Helps you stay on top of garden maintenance

### Export Your Library

Click **"Export CSV"** to download:
- Plant names and locations
- Health status
- Your notes
- Care schedule

## Climate Zone Filter

### Why Climate Zones Matter
Plants grow best within specific UK hardiness zones based on:
- Average winter temperatures
- Frost dates
- Growing season length

### How to Use

1. **Enter your UK postcode**: Type in the input box (e.g., "SW1A 1AA")
2. **Click "Find my zone"**: System looks up your hardiness zone
3. **View your zone**: Displays your UK hardiness zone (H3-H7)
4. **Auto-filter plants**: Only shows plants suitable for your zone
5. **Browse filtered results**: See climate-appropriate plants

### UK Hardiness Zones

| Zone | Winter Temps | Example Regions |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| H1 | Above 15°C | Tropical regions (rare in UK) |
| H2 | 10-15°C | Channel Islands, Southwest |
| H3 | 5-10°C | Southwest England, coastal areas |
| H4 | 0-5°C | Most of England, southern Wales |
| H5 | -5-0°C | North England, northern wales |
| H6 | -10--5°C | Scotland, northern regions |
| H7 | Below -10°C | Extreme northern Scotland |

### Plant Hardiness Info

Each plant shows:
- **Hardiness Zones**: UK zones where it thrives (e.g., "H3-H7")
- **Min/Max Temps**: Lowest/highest temperatures it tolerates
- **Growing Habit**: Will it survive year-round?

## AI Garden Planner

### Purpose
Get personalized plant recommendations based on your garden's conditions and preferences.

### How to Use

1. **Visit**: Click on "AI Garden Planner" from navigation
2. **Fill the form**:
   - Select garden size
   - Choose sunlight level
   - Pick soil type
   - Enter UK postcode (auto-detects zone)
   - Select plant preferences
3. **Submit**: Click "Get Recommendations"
4. **View results**: See suggested plants and layout tips
5. **Browse plants**: Click plant names to see full details in catalogue

### Garden Size Options
- **Small** (~5-10 m²): Containers, small borders
- **Medium** (~10-25 m²): Typical suburban garden
- **Large** (25+ m²): Large gardens, estates

### Sunlight Levels
- **Full Sun**: 6+ hours direct sun
- **Partial Shade**: 3-6 hours sun
- **Shade**: Less than 3 hours sun

### Soil Types
- **Clay**: Heavy, water-retaining soil
- **Sandy**: Light, well-draining soil
- **Loamy**: Balanced, ideal soil
- **Mixed**: Uncertain/varied soil

### Plant Preferences
Select what you want to grow:
- Flowers for color and pollinators
- Herbs for culinary use
- Vegetables for food
- Low-maintenance plants for easy care

### Recommendations Include
- **Plant suggestions**: 10-15 suitable plants
- **Layout tips**: How to arrange plants
- **Companion planting**: Which plants grow well together
- **Care guide**: Watering, feeding, maintenance
- **Dog safety**: Confirms all plants are non-toxic

## Care Schedule

### What It Tracks

The care schedule shows upcoming tasks for plants in your library:

| Task Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Watering | Every 7 days (default) | Keep plants hydrated |
| Fertilizing | Monthly/seasonal | Provide nutrients |
| Pruning | Seasonal | Shape and health |
| Health Check | Monthly | Spot problems early |
| Deadheading | As needed | Encourage flowering |

### Viewing Tasks

Tasks are displayed in priority order:
1. **Overdue tasks** (red) - Do immediately
2. **Due today** (orange) - Do this week
3. **Upcoming** (blue) - Plan ahead

### Adding Care Tasks

1. Add plant to library
2. System creates default watering task
3. Click "Add Task" to create custom reminders
4. Set task type, date, and frequency
5. Receive notifications when due

### Completing Tasks

1. Click the task in Care Schedule
2. Task details appear
3. Click "Mark Complete"
4. Next occurrence is scheduled automatically

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often should I water my plants?
**A**: Each plant shows watering needs in its profile. Generally:
- Summer: 1-2 times per week
- Spring/Fall: Once per week
- Winter: Every 2-3 weeks
- Check soil moisture before watering

### Q: Why does my plant show a placeholder image?
**A**: Some plants don't have verified images yet. You can:
- Click "Find correct photo" to help identify one
- Browse iNaturalist for images
- The admin can update the image database

### Q: Is the plant really safe for dogs?
**A**: Yes! All plants are verified as non-toxic by the ASPCA database. However:
- Dogs should not eat large quantities
- Individual dogs may have allergies
- Always supervise your pet in the garden
- Call vet if pet ingests unknown amount

### Q: Can I grow this plant in my area?
**A**: Use the climate zone filter:
1. Enter your UK postcode
2. Only see plants suited to your zone
3. Check individual plant's hardiness info

### Q: What does "May be Toxic" mean?
**A**: Some plant parts are toxic in large quantities, but safe in small amounts:
- Avoid letting dogs eat them
- Remove fallen leaves/seeds regularly
- Consider planting in fenced areas
- Usually safe if properly supervised

### Q: How do I export my plant library?
**A**: 
1. Go to "My Plant Library" tab
2. Click "Export CSV" button
3. Opens download in Excel/spreadsheet program
4. Use for backup or planning

### Q: Can I access my library from another device?
**A**: Currently, your library is stored locally on your browser. To sync:
- Export your library as CSV
- Import on another device
- Future versions may add cloud sync

### Q: What if I find an error or missing plant?
**A**: 
1. Use the admin dashboard to:
   - Report issues
   - Suggest new plants
   - Submit better images
2. Help improve the database for everyone

### Q: How often is the plant database updated?
**A**: The system automatically:
- Searches for new plant images weekly
- Updates hardiness zone data seasonally
- Adds new plants on request
- Verifies ASPCA safety information continuously
